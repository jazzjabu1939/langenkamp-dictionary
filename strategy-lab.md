# Strategy Lab

## A Business Simulation — Footrace Prototype

Strategy Lab is our independent, working prototype of a business simulation inspired by Capstone’s Footrace format: one player manages a company against five computer-controlled competitors. It includes research and development, marketing, production and finance.

**The simulation currently runs locally on Maxwell, our development computer. It is not yet available to play on this website. We are happy to demonstrate it to interested colleagues.**

To arrange a demonstration, contact [Matthew Langenkamp](mailto:mlangenkamp@umass.edu).

We have reproduced some of the simulation’s functions, but substantial development and validation remain. This is an experimental teaching tool, not a finished replacement for Capstone or a classroom-ready system. Our aim is to explore how a school might adapt a simulation to its own courses, learning objectives and pedagogy.

Strategy Lab is independently developed and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Capsim.
