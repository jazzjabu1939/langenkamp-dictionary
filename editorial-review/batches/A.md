## Batch findings

- **33 entries reviewed:** 16 required no change; 10 received light revisions; 7 received substantive revisions.
- The main prose defect was repeated contrast cadence: `not X / but Y`, paired aphorisms, and short concluding sentences written to make the previous line land.
- The review also found factual or conceptual drift in *Agent*, *Anthropic*, *Apple Silicon*, and *Approximate Turing Machine*.
- Short glossary entries were generally stronger than long reference and essay entries because they defined the term early and stopped when the definition was complete.
- The batch reduced negative-parallelism scanner hits across the whole corpus from 127 to 114. Scanner results were used only for triage.

## Representative changes

### Definition before flourish

*Agent Memory* formerly opened with an aphorism. The definition now comes first; the illustration follows in plain language.

### Rhetoric to operation

*Agentic Engineering* formerly developed an extended code-fountain and plumbing metaphor. It now distinguishes vibe coding from agentic engineering through architecture, constraints, review, testing, deployment, and maintenance.

### Conceptual correction

*Approximate Turing Machine* formerly treated brains and modern AI as the same kind of probabilistic implementation and moved too quickly from computability to eventual capability. It now presents a bounded computational hypothesis and states what computability does not establish.

### Preserved voice

*Agent Town Experiment*, *The Alliance*, *Anchored Persona*, and *Artifactory* retain their central metaphors because those metaphors explain the cases rather than decorate them.
