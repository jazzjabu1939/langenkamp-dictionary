## Batch findings

- **18 entries reviewed:** 8 received light revisions, 9 received substantive revisions, and 1 remained unchanged. Seventeen entry files are revised in this proposal.
- The batch's main recurring defect was architectural overclaim. Hardware ownership was treated as legal compliance; sparse activation was treated as dense-model equivalence; and a useful context-contamination metaphor had leaked into claims about MoE routing. The revisions separate hardware, model architecture, workflow, and governance.
- Three fast-moving institutional entries required current-state repairs. Apple's Mac Studio line is now M5 Max/M5 Ultra; China's block prevented the Meta–Manus acquisition and Manus resumed independent operations; MCP now sits under the Agentic AI Foundation.
- *Mediation (a la Gibson)* retains the operator's voice, Shibuya memory, Gibson lens, humour, and marked Big Call. Its factual scaffolding now uses OpenAI's published advertising terms and recognises that a locally hosted agent may still depend on cloud models and services.
- *Move 37* now states the historically important training sequence: the 2016 AlphaGo learned first from expert games and then through self-play. The entry uses validation and changed expert practice as the term's limiting conditions.
- All cited URLs resolved during the review. Every internal link in the eighteen rendered pages points to a built canonical target, and no rendered M page contains a literal `.md` URL.
- The negative-parallelism scanner reports 87 hits across 156,560 words (0.56 per thousand), down from 89 at the L baseline. No changed M entry appears among the corpus's flagged high-density files.
