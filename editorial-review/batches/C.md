## Batch findings

- **31 entries reviewed:** 12 required no change; 8 received light revisions; 11 received substantive revisions.
- The main editorial risk was factual brittleness rather than ornament alone. Product-version lists, current operator habits, price ratios, and capability rankings age quickly and should appear only when they are historically necessary.
- *The CERN Alternative* contained a source link that failed verification during the review. It now cites CERN's live history page, which quotes the convention.
- *Consciousness Calculator* now states that it estimates the commercial value of attention, expressed intent, and influence. It does not measure consciousness itself.
- *Cooperative Writing* remains the longest entry in the batch. Its examples and teaching application were retained, but repeated formulations of the same distinction were removed.
- The new stock-transition rule was applied contextually. No C revision uses “that distinction matters” or replaces it with a rotating synonym merely for variety.

## Representative changes

### Evidence before extrapolation

*Capability Overhang* no longer asserts a general percentage of model capability used by customers. It describes the observable lag between technical capability and tested institutional use.

### Historical concept and Dictionary extension

*Cheng* now defines the Confucian term in its established sense before explaining the Dictionary's narrower use: alignment among judgment, expression, and action.

### Metaphor with a boundary

*Consciousness Calculator* retains the Silverado and loneliness-ad examples but identifies the calculator as an adjustable economic model. The metaphor no longer substitutes for the quantity being estimated.

### Product entries that can age

The Claude-family entries now explain durable tier and interface roles. Version numbers and historical prices remain only where they explain a dated decision.
