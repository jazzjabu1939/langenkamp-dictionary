**Not Batteries, Not Monks** is the Dictionary’s middle path for AI sovereignty.

Nobody wants to become a battery: a resource harvested by a system whose purposes are not one’s own. But unplugging entirely is not the answer either. The goal is not to flee into a cave and congratulate oneself on purity while the world’s intelligence systems are built elsewhere.

The goal is disciplined participation: use powerful tools, build local capacity, keep memory and workflows as sovereign as possible, and refuse both passive dependence and performative abstention.

This is why the *[Buddha Stack](buddha-stack.md)* is a stack rather than a monastery.
