DeepSeek is a Chinese AI lab founded in July 2023 by Liang Wenfeng as a research arm of the Hangzhou-based quantitative hedge fund High-Flyer. The lab is headquartered in Hangzhou and Beijing. It has released a series of open-weights large language models — most prominently *DeepSeek-V3* (a 671B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts general-purpose model) and *DeepSeek-R1* (one of the first widely-distributed open-weights **reasoning models* *, released January 2025, that triggered substantial market reaction including a one-day drop in US tech-stock valuations).

For this Dictionary, DeepSeek matters at two levels. First, **technically**: DeepSeek's models are credible options in the *[Sovereign Compute](sovereign-compute.md)* tier, especially for operators who need reasoning capability at open-weights cost. Second, **strategically**: DeepSeek is one of the most legible signals that the PRC's AI ecosystem is producing frontier-adjacent capability with materially different cost structures from the US labs. The Dictionary's *[Capability Overhang](capability-overhang.md)* argument and the in-progress *[zhengming](https://github.com/jazzjabu1939/langenkamp-dictionary)* (正名) essay both treat DeepSeek as structurally important.

The lab's relationship to the Chinese state — described in DeepSeek's own statements as that of an independent private research lab — is the subject of ongoing analysis the Dictionary may eventually address in a future entry.
