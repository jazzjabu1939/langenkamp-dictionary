DeepSeek is a Chinese AI company founded in 2023 by Liang Wenfeng and based in Hangzhou. High-Flyer, the quantitative hedge fund also founded by Liang, funded the company. DeepSeek has released downloadable model weights, an API, and a consumer chat service.

Its best-known early releases were *DeepSeek-V3*, a mixture-of-experts model with 671 billion total parameters and 37 billion activated for each token, and *DeepSeek-R1*, a January 2025 *[reasoning model](/entries/reasoning-model/)* trained through a combination of reinforcement learning and supervised fine-tuning. DeepSeek released the weights for both models and several smaller models distilled from R1.

For this Dictionary, DeepSeek matters technically and strategically. Its releases give operators additional options for *[Sovereign Compute](/entries/sovereign-compute/)* and demonstrate that a Chinese lab can produce models competitive with leading US systems while distributing the weights. Downloadable weights do not make a 671-billion-parameter model easy or cheap to run; the smaller distilled models make local use more practical.
