The **Buckley Amendment** is the common historical name for the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act of 1974, or FERPA. It established student rights over educational records and placed privacy obligations on institutions that receive federal funds.

For AI systems in education, the Buckley Amendment matters because the legal object is not *AI* as such. The legal object is the student record, the institution's handling of it, and whether protected information is disclosed to unauthorised parties or vendors without proper basis. A chatbot becomes legally interesting when student data passes through it.

The Dictionary's *FERPA Compliance Posture* entry argues that local models on controlled hardware can be compliance infrastructure, not merely privacy preference. The Buckley Amendment is the legal root underneath that argument.
