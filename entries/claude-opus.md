Claude Opus is the high-capability tier in *[Anthropic](anthropic.md)*'s *[Claude](claude.md)* model family. Anthropic positions it for difficult reasoning, writing, coding, and agentic work, usually at a higher price and latency than the *[Sonnet](claude-sonnet.md)* tier. The exact benchmark lead and price ratio change by model generation.

Claude Opus was the principal drafting model for much of this Dictionary's early development. *[Opus Addict](opus-addict.md)* names the resulting dependence on a closed frontier tier and the operational risks that remain even when the model performs well.

A 16-day cost review in April 2026 found that cache writes accounted for 72.6% of the operator's Anthropic spending, while output tokens accounted for 4.8%. That result helped motivate the local-compute architecture described in *[Sovereign Compute](sovereign-compute.md)*.
