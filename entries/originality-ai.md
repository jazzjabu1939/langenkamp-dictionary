Originality.ai is an AI-detection and plagiarism-checking tool launched in 2022 by founder Jonathan Gillham. The product combines a traditional plagiarism check (comparing submitted text against a large web corpus for unoriginal copy) with an AI-detection classifier (claiming to identify AI-generated text). The company's primary market is content marketing and SEO — agencies and publishers who want to verify that contracted writers are producing human work — with secondary markets in academic-integrity and editorial review.

For this Dictionary, Originality.ai is one of the named players in the **AI-detection economy** discussed in [The Olang' Trap](/entries/olang-trap/) and [The Sinceerly Stack](/entries/sinceerly-stack/). Research has found serious false-positive and cross-population problems in AI-text detectors as a class; that evidence should not be silently converted into a product-specific finding about every current version of Originality.ai. The operational conclusion is narrower: no detector score should, by itself, decide authorship or misconduct.

Originality.ai says it was launched in November 2022 for web publishers and content marketers. It now markets AI detection, plagiarism checking, fact checking, readability tools, and related editorial features. Those are the company's claims and product categories, not an independent validation of accuracy.
