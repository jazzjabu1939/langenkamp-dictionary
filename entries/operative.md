**The Operative** is the Alliance agent in *Serenity* who pursues Malcolm Reynolds and the crew after they discover the truth about Miranda.

He is frightening because he is not stupid, not buffoonish, and not personally sadistic. He is calm, literate, disciplined, and morally serious. He knows the system he serves does terrible things. He knows he himself is one of those terrible things. But he believes the suffering is justified because the Alliance is building “a better world.”

For the Dictionary, the Operative is the emblem of morally serious institutional violence: the person who has subordinated his conscience to a theory of order and still retains enough conscience to know what he has done.

This is why he belongs near AI governance. The risk is not only cartoon villainy. The risk is competent benevolence with instruments.
