Nous Research is a decentralised open-source AI research collective founded in 2023 by a small group of independent researchers including Karan Malhotra, Bowen Peng, and Teknium (a pseudonymous figure who has been one of the most visible open-source-AI contributors of the era). The collective is best known for the *[Hermes](hermes.md)* family of fine-tuned models — instruction-following adaptations of open-weights base models (typically Llama) — and for a sustained body of post-training research that has been influential well beyond the collective's own releases.

For this Dictionary, Nous Research matters as one of the most consequential **independent open-weights labs** in the 2025–2026 ecosystem. Where Mistral and Meta release base models, Nous fine-tunes and post-trains those base models into something more directly useful for operators running local inference. Hermes is, in practice, the default candidate the Dictionary's *[Sovereign Compute](sovereign-compute.md)* calculator recommends for operators who want a known-quantity instruction-tuned local model without doing the fine-tuning themselves.

The collective's funding model (a mix of grants, independent compute, and occasional commercial partnerships) is itself a Dictionary-relevant data point: a working alternative to the lab-as-startup paradigm.
