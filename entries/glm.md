# GLM

**GLM is the General Language Model family developed from a Tsinghua University pretraining line and commercialized by Zhipu AI / Z.ai.**

The original GLM research combined autoregressive generation with blank-infilling objectives: the model learned both to continue text and to reconstruct missing spans. Later ChatGLM and GLM generations expanded into conversational, coding, multimodal, and agentic systems.

GLM-5.2 became strategically important in mid-2026 because it joined capable open-weight distribution with serious cybersecurity performance. NIST's CAISI assessment found its aggregate cyber capability similar to Claude Opus 4.6, released several months earlier, while still significantly below the strongest closed U.S. models available when the assessment was published. That is a *[Benchmark Parity](benchmark-parity.md)* lesson: a narrowing gap in a consequential domain, not evidence that every capability had equalized.
