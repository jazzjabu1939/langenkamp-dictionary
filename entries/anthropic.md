Anthropic is an AI company founded in 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and other former OpenAI staff. The company develops the *[Claude](claude.md)* family of large language models (*[Opus](claude-opus.md)*, *[Sonnet](claude-sonnet.md)*, and *[Haiku](claude-haiku.md)*) and developed the *[Constitutional AI](constitutional-ai.md)* training approach. It is headquartered in San Francisco; major investors include Amazon and Google.

For this Dictionary, Anthropic is an important frontier-model provider and the source of models used extensively in the operator's earlier agentic work. The *[Opus Addict](opus-addict.md)* entry describes the capability gains and single-vendor dependence that accompanied that use. The *[Lab Character](lab-character.md)* entry treats Anthropic as a worked example of an AI lab whose stated mission and commercial trajectory remain in tension.
