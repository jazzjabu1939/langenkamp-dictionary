Anthropic is an AI safety company founded in 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and several other former OpenAI staff who left over disagreements about the pace and orientation of frontier AI development. The lab develops the *[Claude](claude.md)* family of large language models (*[Opus](claude-opus.md)*, *[Sonnet](claude-sonnet.md)*, *[Haiku](claude-haiku.md)*) and the broader *[Constitutional AI](constitutional-ai.md)* training paradigm. Headquartered in San Francisco; major investors include Google, Amazon, and Salesforce.

For this Dictionary, Anthropic is the **frontier-model provider the operator runs**. The *[Opus Addict](opus-addict.md)* entry names the dependency on Anthropic's most-capable tier as a structural feature of careful agentic work in 2026, with both the upside (sustained capability gains across the 4.x series) and the downside (single-vendor reliance) acknowledged. The *[Lab Character](lab-character.md)* entry treats Anthropic as one of the worked examples of an AI lab whose stated mission and commercial trajectory are in productive but unresolved tension.

The xAI/Anthropic data centre deal announced May 7, 2026 is one of several recent structural moves the Dictionary may revisit in future entries.
