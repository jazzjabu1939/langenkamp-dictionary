An **agentic loop** is the repeating cycle in which an agent plans a next step, acts through a tool, observes the result, verifies what happened, and decides whether to continue, correct, escalate, or stop.

The loop is what separates an agent from a chatbot. A chatbot produces an answer. An agent can let one result become evidence for the next action:

**plan → act → observe → verify → continue, correct, escalate, or stop**

Repetition alone does not make the loop dependable. Without verification, a mistaken observation becomes the premise for the next action. Without stopping conditions, persistence becomes wandering. Without escalation, the agent may keep attempting work after it has reached the edge of its authority or competence.

The harness gives the loop its practical shape: which tools the agent may use, what results it can observe, what counts as success, how much time or money it may spend, when approval is required, and how failure becomes visible. The model supplies the next-step judgment; the harness supplies the road, the guardrails, and the exits.
