In OpenClaw, **onboarding** is the guided, step-by-step setup that runs after installation. It asks you to choose a [model](/entries/model/) connection, tests that connection with a real request, sets up the [workspace](/entries/workspace/), and starts the [Gateway](/entries/gateway/). The standard installer starts it automatically; the command `openclaw onboard` runs it again.

Onboarding offers two routes. **Quick start** detects available AI access and moves quickly with default settings. **Custom setup** walks through each choice, including how much the assistant may do. The Dictionary's guide recommends Custom setup for a first installation so that you choose the access settings yourself.

In ordinary business use, *onboarding* means bringing a new employee or customer into an organization. The software meaning borrows the idea: a structured first encounter that sets up how the relationship will work.
