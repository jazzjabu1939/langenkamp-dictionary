**Mandi Loop** is a companion name for the **[Mandi Step](/entries/mandi-step/)**: the repair loop in which a human notices that an automated workflow is about to damage trust, adds relationship context, and returns the situation to human judgment before the system's formally correct action becomes socially wrong.

The Dictionary's preferred term is **Mandi Step**, because the load-bearing act is the human step into the loop. But *Mandi Loop* is useful when emphasizing the cycle: system signal → human recognition → relationship repair → system redesign.
