**Resource Visibility** is the principle that AI’s physical costs should be visible enough for the operator to manage them: electricity, heat, hardware depreciation, cooling, water, grid load, and the opportunity cost of calling a frontier model when a smaller local model would do.

Cloud AI makes intelligence feel frictionless. Ask, receive, ask again. The data center, cooling system, power plant, rare-earth supply chain, and capital budget disappear behind the interface. The bill may show up as a subscription, but the physical act has been abstracted away.

Local or partly local AI changes the moral posture. The workstation gets warm. The fan spins. The battery drains. The solar meter moves. The power bill changes. The operator sees, at least in miniature, that intelligence has a physical cost.

What is measured gets managed. Resource Visibility is not a complete answer to AI’s energy problem, and individual virtue is not infrastructure policy. But a free society should prefer systems that help individuals see some part of the cost of their own capability.

Freedom without measurement becomes fantasy. Measurement without freedom becomes administration. The interesting zone is freedom disciplined by visible consequences.
