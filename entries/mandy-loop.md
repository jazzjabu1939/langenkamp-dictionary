**Mandy Loop** is a companion name for the **[Mandy Step](mandy-step.md)**: the repair loop in which a human notices that an automated workflow is about to damage trust, adds relationship context, and returns the situation to human judgment before the system's formally correct action becomes socially wrong.

The Dictionary's preferred term is **Mandy Step**, because the load-bearing act is the human step into the loop. But *Mandy Loop* is useful when emphasizing the cycle: system signal → human recognition → relationship repair → system redesign.
