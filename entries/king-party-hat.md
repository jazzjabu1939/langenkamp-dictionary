King Party Hat is the operator's name for the error of putting an under-governed local model at the top of an agent stack and mistaking local ownership for actual authority architecture.

The phrase comes from *[The Experimental Party](/entries/experimental-party/)*. A local model may be private, cheap, and fast, but those virtues do not by themselves make it fit to govern tools, memory, external actions, or other agents. The more consequential the action, the more the system needs an appropriate harness, permissions, and an independent control boundary. Sovereignty is not the same thing as handing the crown to the nearest process.

The correction is not anti-local. It is pro-architecture: orchestration, approval gates, judge layers, logging, recovery, and clear authority boundaries.
