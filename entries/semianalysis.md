**SemiAnalysis** is the semiconductor and AI-infrastructure research publication founded by *[Dylan Patel](/entries/dylan-patel/)*.

For this Dictionary, SemiAnalysis is important because it keeps the AI conversation attached to the physical substrate: GPUs, high-bandwidth memory, advanced packaging, TSMC allocation, data-center power, networking, cooling, and the capital structure of hyperscale buildouts.

This is a necessary antidote to frictionless AI talk. The chatbot window hides the industrial stack. SemiAnalysis studies the stack.
