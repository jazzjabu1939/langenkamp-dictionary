**Riley Brown** is a video creator who demonstrates AI tools and practical ways to use them. His walkthroughs give viewers concrete examples of what they might build with an assistant, a shared workspace, or an agent connected to other services.

In his video [“ChatGPT Just Changed A LOT (Here's What's New)”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fSilBzYdHUw), Brown presents OpenAI's announcements enthusiastically. We read his coverage here as strongly pro-OpenAI: it emphasizes the appeal and possibilities of the new tools. That is our assessment of this presentation, not a claim about employment, sponsorship, or a commercial relationship with OpenAI.

His demonstrations are useful starting points for experiments. Viewers should distinguish features shown working from those described as forthcoming, and consider costs, permissions, and the difficulty of moving their work elsewhere. Our essay *Get Ready to Get Pregnant: AI agents, attachment, and the price of leaving* grew out of those questions after reviewing this video.

Brown also appears in our [Grok Bot](/entries/grok-bot/) entry, through his demonstration of an assistant called Jimmy. His coverage is therefore not confined to OpenAI.
