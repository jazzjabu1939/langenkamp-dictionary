Gemini is the proprietary model family produced by *[Google DeepMind](/entries/google-deepmind/)*. Google announced the first generation in December 2023 in three sizes: Ultra, Pro, and Nano. Later releases expanded the family across long-context, multimodal, reasoning, and agentic uses; Gemini 1.5 introduced a context window of up to one million tokens, and the Gemini 3 series was current in 2026.

For this Dictionary, Gemini matters at three levels: as a peer of *[GPT](/entries/gpt/)* and *[Claude](/entries/claude/)* in the closed-model market; as the proprietary sibling of the open-weights *[Gemma](/entries/gemma/)* line; and as the model family Google integrates into consumer products and its Vertex AI platform. The name therefore refers both to models and to the consumer application built around them. Keeping those layers separate prevents product features from being mistaken for properties of one model.
