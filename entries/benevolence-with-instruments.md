**Benevolence with Instruments** is the dangerous combination of sincere good intentions, institutional power, technical tools, and permission to redesign human systems at scale.

It is frightening precisely because it is not ordinary villainy. The person holding the instrument may be calm, serious, literate, safety-conscious, and morally motivated. The problem is that once benevolence acquires enough instruments — data centers, models, policies, platforms, funding, state access, atmospheric agents on Miranda — it can stop hearing the people being improved.

The phrase comes from the Dictionary’s *Firefly* / *Serenity* reading of the Operative: not malice, but benevolence with instruments.
