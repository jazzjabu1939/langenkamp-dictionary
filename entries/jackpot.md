Across William Gibson's *Jackpot* sequence — *[The Peripheral](/entries/the-peripheral/)* (2014) and *Agency* (2020) — the *jackpot* is the historical process that has, by the far-future setting, already happened. It is a confluence: pandemics, climate change, ecosystem collapse, antibiotic resistance, financial cascades, demographic crises, and political failure, unfolding across decades and compounding into a population reduction of roughly eighty per cent. The name is sardonic: the machine pays out all at once, except what it pays is catastrophe.

Gibson's choice of name does the work of the concept. We tend to imagine collapse as event-shaped because our narrative training is event-shaped. The *jackpot* is collapse as system-shaped: every crisis is real, every crisis worsens every other, no single one is decisive. By the time it can be named, it has already happened.

In this Dictionary the term is the structural ancestor of several arguments: see *[Aunties](/entries/aunties/)* for Gibson's post-jackpot AI-assistance regime; *[Sixfold Skyreading](/entries/sixfold-skyreading/)* and *[Grey Swans](/entries/grey-swans/)* for convergence among causes; and *[Mediation (a la Gibson)](/entries/mediation-a-la-gibson/)* for the operator's use of Gibson as a design source. Gibson's own gloss, from *The Peripheral*, is exact: *"It was multicausal, with no particular beginning and no end. More a climate than an event."*
