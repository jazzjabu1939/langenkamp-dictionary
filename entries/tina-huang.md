**Tina Huang** is an ex-Meta data scientist, YouTube educator, and entrepreneur whose public work focuses on AI, coding, data science, career development, and self-study. Her channel is one of the more student-friendly AI channels because she tends to demonstrate workflows rather than merely announce tools. It is useful to this Dictionary less as a source of theory than as a source of teachable practice: how a reasonably technical person actually uses new AI tools to study, build, research, and make decisions.

That distinction matters for students. Much AI commentary either floats above practice or collapses into product hype. Huang is often better when the assignment is: *watch someone use the tool, notice the sequence of decisions, and then adapt the workflow for your own problem*. Her *[NotebookLM](notebooklm.md)* walkthrough is a good example. The educational value is not simply that NotebookLM can generate slides, podcasts, reports, and videos. It is that she begins with source quality, states constraints, adds validation sources, compares options, and turns the notebook into multiple artifacts.

For Prof. Langenkamp's Fall 2026 students, Huang belongs on the "useful practitioner" shelf: not a final authority, not a substitute for judgment, but a clear demonstrator of AI-assisted work habits.
