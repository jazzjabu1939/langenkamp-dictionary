**Nate Jones** is an AI strategist and commentator whose daily AI-news and strategy content sits in the Dictionary's source ecosystem: not as an academic authority, but as one of the practical interpreters of the fast-moving AI-product layer. His own public description emphasises AI strategy, product leadership, and cutting through hype with workflows and frameworks.

For the Dictionary, Jones is useful because the operator often brings transcripts from this genre into the evening review process. The work then is not mere summary. It is triage: what is genuinely new, what is click heat, what should change our local system, and what belongs nowhere near the Dictionary.

He is therefore a named marker for a media habit as much as a person-entry: the nightly AI-watch loop.
