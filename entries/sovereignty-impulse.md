Sovereignty Impulse is the moment an operator decides that convenience is no longer enough and starts building local, owned, inspectable infrastructure for work that matters.

It is not necessarily anti-cloud, anti-lab, or anti-subscription. The impulse is more specific: some workflows are too personal, too regulated, too strategic, or too identity-bearing to leave entirely inside someone else's product boundary.

The sovereignty impulse often begins as irritation: a login breaks, a policy shifts, a model changes, a platform refuses the documented path, a bill becomes opaque. Properly handled, the irritation becomes architecture.
