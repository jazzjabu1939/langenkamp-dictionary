# Zhipu AI / Z.ai

**Zhipu AI (智谱) is the Beijing artificial-intelligence company behind the GLM model family. The name Z.ai is short for Zhipu AI and serves as the company's international-facing brand.**

The company emerged from research associated with Tsinghua University's Knowledge Engineering Group and has become one of China's most important model developers. Its GLM family spans language, coding, agentic, and multimodal systems, with both hosted services and open-weight releases.

For the Dictionary, Zhipu matters because it makes the *[Open-Weights Inversion](open-weights-inversion.md)* concrete. In 2026, GLM-5.2 showed that security-relevant capability comparable to earlier U.S. frontier models could appear in a downloadable Chinese model. That changed the policy problem from controlling a few American APIs to governing a capability that could circulate at the weight level.

Z.ai followed it with **GLM-5.3**, the company's latest flagship model. GLM-5.3 uses the same base model as GLM-5.2, with its gains coming from post-training. Z.ai reports substantially stronger performance in complex software engineering, long-horizon agent work, and cybersecurity tasks, including a 50 percent improvement over GLM-5.2 on the company's own Z.ai Code Bench. The model is text-only, reasons by default, and supports a context window of up to one million tokens. The release sharpens the strategic point already visible in GLM-5.2: Chinese model developers are not merely narrowing benchmark gaps; they are competing directly in coding and agentic work, where models increasingly act on systems rather than simply answer questions.

The names should not be collapsed carelessly. **Zhipu AI** is the company name used in China and in much of its history. **Z.ai** is the international brand. **GLM** is the model family.
