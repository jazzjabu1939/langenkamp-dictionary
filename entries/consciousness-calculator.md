## In one sentence

**The *Consciousness Calculator* is a Dictionary tool that estimates the commercial value of the attention, expressed intent, and influence over later choices that a user may exchange for a free AI service.**

## How much are your thoughts worth?

If I were to say *a nickel for your thoughts*, would that be enough? The calculator begins with the claim that a free service may receive something commercially valuable from the exchange, even when the user pays no subscription fee.

Consider two hypothetical ads. A user asks about changing the oil in a Chevy Silverado 2500HD and sees an advertisement for an AutoZone oil filter. The advertisement responds to a practical purchasing intention.

Now suppose the user describes loneliness on a Friday night and sees an advertisement from an online pharmacy for an antidepressant. The second placement uses a more vulnerable disclosure and may influence how the user interprets a personal condition. The two impressions may have different commercial value and different human consequences.

The calculator uses *consciousness* as a deliberately broad name for this combination of attention, disclosed intent, and possible influence. It does not claim that consciousness itself has been measured. Its purpose is to make a normally implicit commercial exchange available for inspection.

## What it actually estimates

The estimate uses:

- Hours per day spent in the service.
- Approximate sessions per week.
- Demographic and economic profile relevant to advertiser bidding (geography, age range, household income bracket, professional category).
- The advertising rates the service's most likely buyers are paying in adjacent attention markets (Google search ads, Meta ads, programmatic display).
- An adjustable multiplier for the possibility that an AI assistant can influence what the user does next.

The output is a dollar estimate per hour, day, week, and month. The result depends on contestable assumptions about advertising rates and influence. It should not be read as a market quotation or as evidence that a platform actually earns that amount from a particular user.

## Why the visibility matters more than the precision

Many users of free services do not assign a value to the attention and information exchanged during use. The calculator turns assumptions about that exchange into visible inputs and an estimated result. A user can inspect the assumptions, reject them, or compare the estimate with a paid subscription.

The tool does not establish that a paid or local service is always the better choice. It supplies one input for that decision alongside price, privacy terms, model quality, convenience, and the user's sensitivity to profiling or advertising.

The [Sovereign Compute Calculator](sovereign-compute-calculator.md) estimates the cash breakeven for local-first infrastructure. The two tools place different costs beside each other: the estimated value exchanged with a service and the cash cost of operating an alternative.

## Status

**Live as of May 7, 2026.** The calculator is published at [/tools/consciousness-calculator/](/tools/consciousness-calculator/). It runs in the browser, holds no state on a server, and makes no outbound network requests after page load. Its “How we computed this” section states the formulas and assumptions.
