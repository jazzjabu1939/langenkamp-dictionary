**Using the Empire’s Telescope** names the sovereignty paradox: we use frontier systems built by hyperscaler AI companies to search for ways of reducing dependence on those same systems.

The phrase is not a purity accusation. It is an honesty device. In 2026, the best frontier models from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and their peers can do intellectual work that local models still cannot match. Anyone serious about AI sovereignty has to admit this. The empire’s telescope sees farther.

But temporary use is not the same as permanent residence. The point is to use frontier capability deliberately while building local capacity, durable memory, independent tools, and open-weight workflows that reduce the number of things only the empire can do.

The practical translation is simple: do not waste frontier intelligence on shovel work. Use it for architecture, judgment, synthesis, and hard review. Let local systems handle routine private work wherever they are good enough.
