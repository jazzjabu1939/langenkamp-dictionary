In *Firefly* / *Serenity*, **the Alliance** is the central governing order: bureaucratic, technological, military, and convinced of its own civilizing mission.

For the Dictionary, the Alliance is useful because it is not merely cackling evil. It is competent managerial power. It offers order, safety, hygiene, standardization, and improvement. That is what makes it a better metaphor for many modern institutions than a black-caped villain would be.

The Alliance becomes dangerous when it treats human friction as a defect to be engineered away. Miranda is the result: a better-world experiment that pacifies most of a planet into death and creates the Reavers as its violent remainder.

The warning is not “all governance is tyranny.” The warning is narrower and more useful: institutions with enough instruments can confuse their model of human flourishing with human flourishing itself.
