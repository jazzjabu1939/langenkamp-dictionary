# Project Agora

## The signal

The BIS and IIF said Project Agora's prototype demonstrated atomic settlement using tokenised central-bank reserves and commercial-bank deposits across currencies and jurisdictions, and that the project would advance toward real-value testing.

This matters because payment rails are institutional authority in operational form. Stablecoins, sanctions architecture, correspondent-banking delays, and geopolitical fragmentation all pressure the old monetary channels. Agora is the central-bank system trying to rebuild the rails before others set the terms.

## The reading

The Court reads Project Agora as a quiet institutional answer to a loud monetary problem. The important phrase is not "prototype." It is "real-value testing." That phrase is not deployment, but it creates a scoreable next step: named corridors, named banks, and public boundaries around live-value testing.

If the bone is right, the public record will move from proof-of-concept language toward implementation architecture before the end of October.

## The Jester's counter

The strongest counter is that central banks are masters of the ceremonial pilot. "Real-value testing" may mean a very small sandbox, wrapped in confidentiality, with enough legal caution to keep operational details unnamed until the next report. The BIS can advance the work without giving the Historian a clean public object to score.
